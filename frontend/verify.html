Verify a title deed

Before you pay for land or a house, check that the title deed number the seller gave you matches a property that is genuinely listed on NestKey.

Type the title deed / parcel number exactly as it appears on the document the seller shared.
What this check does — and what it doesn't

This tool only tells you whether the deed number is attached to a listing on NestKey. It is not a connection to the Ministry of Lands and is not proof of legal ownership.

Always confirm land before paying by:

  • Doing an official title search on eCitizen / Ardhisasa or at your local Lands registry.
  • Checking the seller's national ID matches the name on the title.
  • Using a licensed lawyer or surveyor for the transfer.
  • Never sending money for land you have not independently verified.
