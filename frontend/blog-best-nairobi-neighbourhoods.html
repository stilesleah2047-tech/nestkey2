Best Nairobi Neighbourhoods to Live In

Nairobi is really many cities in one, and the right neighbourhood depends on your budget, commute and lifestyle. Here's a quick tour of popular areas to help you choose...then browse live listings in any of them on NestKey.

Kilimani & Kileleshwa

Central, leafy and full of apartments, restaurants and offices. Popular with young professionals who want short commutes to Westlands and the CBD. Expect higher rents but great convenience.

Westlands & Parklands

The commercial heart for many businesses, with nightlife, malls and a mix of apartments. Great if you work nearby and value being in the middle of everything.

Lavington & Kileleshwa

Quieter, upmarket and green town houses and larger apartments suited to families who still want to be close to the centre.

South B & South C

Established, well‑connected and family‑friendly, with good value compared to the western suburbs and easy access to the CBD and the airport.

Langata & Karen

More space and greenery, from apartments in Langata to standalone homes in Karen. Ideal if you want a calmer, suburban feel.

Roysambu, Kasarani & Thika Road

Fast‑growing and affordable, popular with students and young families thanks to the superhighway and plentiful new apartments.

Embakasi, Donholm & Utawala

Eastlands value with good access to the airport and industrial area a lot of housing at friendly prices.

Satellite towns: Kitengela, Ruiru, Ngong, Rongai

Beyond the city you'll find more space for your money great for buying land or renting a bigger home if you don't mind a longer commute.
