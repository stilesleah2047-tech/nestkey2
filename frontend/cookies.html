Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2026

This policy explains the cookies and similar technologies (like your browser's local storage) that NestKey uses. By using NestKey you agree to the use of the essential items described below; you can accept or limit the rest using the banner shown on your first visit, or by clearing site data in your browser.

What we use

NameTypePurpose
hh_tokenStrictly necessaryKeeps you securely signed in to your account.
hh_userStrictly necessaryRemembers your basic profile so the app can show the right dashboard.
nk_cookie_consentStrictly necessaryRemembers your cookie choice so we don't ask again.

Strictly necessary

These are required for the site to work — for example, to keep you signed in and to remember your cookie choice. The site cannot function properly without them.

Functional

These support features like the map view. Maps are loaded from OpenStreetMap, which may set its own technical data as part of serving map tiles.

What we do NOT use

NestKey does not use advertising or cross‑site tracking cookies, and we do not sell your data to advertisers.

Managing cookies

You can clear or block cookies and local storage in your browser settings at any time. Note that removing the strictly‑necessary items will sign you out and reset your preferences. To review this in more detail, see our Privacy Policy.
