Cookie Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This page explains what NestKey stores on your device and which outside services your browser talks to when you use the site. In short: we only store what the site needs to work, and we use no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies.

Cookies and “local storage”

Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser. NestKey mostly uses local storage, a similar feature that keeps small pieces of information on your device. Unlike cookies, it isn’t sent to our servers with every request. This policy covers both.

What NestKey stores on your device

All of these are essential (strictly necessary) and first-party (set by nestkey.co.ke):

NameWhat it doesHow long it stays
hh_tokenKeeps you signed in. It holds a secure sign-in token, not your password.Until you sign out; the token stops working after 30 days
hh_userYour name, email and account type, so the site can show your dashboard and fill in forms for you.Until you sign out
nk_favsThe homes you’ve saved with the heart button.Until you remove them or clear your browser data
nk_cookie_consentRemembers that you’ve seen the cookie notice, so it doesn’t show again.Until you clear your browser data
App cacheIf you install NestKey as an app, copies of some pages and files are kept so the app loads quickly.Until the app updates or you remove it

We don’t use these to track you across other websites or to build advertising profiles. Because they are essential, the site can’t work properly without them, and we don’t ask for consent before setting them. The notice you see on your first visit is there to tell you about them.

Some parts of a page are loaded from other companies. When that happens, your browser sends them your IP address and basic browser details. Some of them may set their own cookies, which their own privacy policies cover.

What we don’t use

  • No advertising cookies
  • No analytics or tracking tools (such as Google Analytics or Facebook Pixel)
  • No cross-site tracking
  • We never sell your data

If we ever add something like this, we’ll update this page first and ask for your consent before it runs.

How to manage or remove them

You can delete NestKey’s stored data at any time:

  • Sign out to remove hh_token and hh_user.
  • To remove everything, open your browser’s settings, find Site settings or Privacy, and clear the site data for nestkey.co.ke.
  • You can also block cookies and site data for NestKey or for any of the services above. If you block NestKey’s own storage, you won’t be able to stay signed in or save homes.

Your rights and contact

Our Privacy Policy explains your rights over your personal data, including how to complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner. For questions about this page, email support@nestkey.co.ke.
