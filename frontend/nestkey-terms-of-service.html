NestKey never holds funds. When a buyer chooses to pay, they see the seller's own payment details and pay the seller directly; the buyer then confirms payment on the Platform (“I’ve paid”), the sale is recorded, and the seller is invoiced the applicable commission, shown as commission owed in their dashboard and settled via M‑Pesa or Pesapal. Any sale, transfer, deposit or payment for a property is a transaction solely between buyer and seller; NestKey is not a party to it and does not guarantee it.
