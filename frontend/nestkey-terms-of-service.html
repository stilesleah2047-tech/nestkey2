8. Property sales & commission

For properties listed for sale (including land), NestKey operates a hybrid model: the seller needs an active plan to list, and NestKey earns a commission of 8% (or the then-current rate) of the sale price when the property is paid for.

8.1 NestKey never holds funds

NestKey does not operate an escrow or hold buyer funds. When a buyer chooses to pay for a property, they see the seller's own payment details and pay the seller directly. The buyer then confirms payment on the Platform (“I’ve paid”), the sale is recorded, and the seller is invoiced the applicable commission, shown as commission owed in their dashboard and settled via M-Pesa or Pesapal.

8.2 Transactions are between users

Any sale, transfer, deposit, or payment for a property is a transaction solely between the buyer and the seller. NestKey is not a party to it, does not guarantee it, and is not responsible for the conduct, solvency, title, or performance of either party. You are strongly advised to carry out independent due diligence, use a qualified advocate, and verify title and ownership before paying any money.

9. Rentals

For properties listed for rent, NestKey operates on a subscription-only basis: landlords pay a plan to publish within their quota, and NestKey charges no commission on rent.
