About NestKey

Kenya's simpler way to find and list homes, rentals and land.

Our mission

NestKey exists to make finding a home in Kenya faster, clearer and more trustworthy. Whether you're a hunter looking for a bedsitter in Roysambu, a family buying land in Kitengela, or an agent managing a portfolio, NestKey connects the right people with the right property...by county, by town, and pinned to the exact spot on a map.

Our story

NestKey was born out of a frustration every Kenyan house hunter knows too well: endless WhatsApp forwards, blurry photos, “viewing fees” for houses that don't exist, and locations described only as “near the shops.” Finding a home shouldn't feel like a gamble. We set out to build a single, honest place where a listing shows you exactly what you're getting — real photos, real videos, a real map pin — and where paying is as simple as a familiar M‑Pesa prompt. What started as a better way to search quickly became a better way to list too, giving landlords and agents the tools to reach serious, ready renters and buyers without the noise.

What you can do on NestKey

  • Search by area — pick a county and town, or type any area, and see everything listed there.
  • See it properly — multiple photos, room‑by‑room video showcases, and an exact map location with directions.
  • List with confidence — list a single property with a small one-off fee (from KSh 100 per room), or choose a monthly plan if you manage several properties.
  • Pay securely — subscriptions and payments run on M‑Pesa and card via Pesapal.
  • Enquire instantly — message or WhatsApp a lister directly, with their details right on the listing.
  • Filter what matters — narrow by price, bedrooms, rent or sale, and sort to find the right fit in seconds.

How it works

House hunters browse and enquire for free. Landlords, agents and individuals create an account, add their properties with photos, videos and a map pin, and publish them either with a one-off listing fee (from KSh 100 per room) or on a monthly plan that fits their portfolio. For sales, an 8% commission applies only when a property actually sells — renting stays commission‑free.

Who NestKey is for

  • House hunters — students, families and professionals searching for the right place in the right neighbourhood.
  • Landlords — individual property owners who want to fill vacancies quickly with genuine tenants.
  • Agents & agencies — professionals managing many listings who need a clean, powerful portfolio dashboard.

Why people choose NestKey

  • Real listings only — we show genuine, uploaded photos and videos, never stock or recycled images.
  • Honest locations — every listing is pinned to an exact spot on the map, so “near the shops” becomes a real address.
  • Fair pricing — a small one-off fee or a simple monthly plan for listers, free browsing for hunters, and no commission on rentals.
  • Payments you trust — M‑Pesa and card via Pesapal, with money for sales going directly between buyer and seller.
  • Built around Kenya — county‑and‑town search, local payment habits, and a design that works on any phone.

Trust & safety

Your safety matters to us. NestKey is a marketplace that connects people — we never hold your rent or sale money, and we encourage every user to view a property in person and verify ownership before paying anything. We work to keep listings genuine, flag suspicious activity, and give you the lister's real contact details so you can deal with a real person. If something doesn't look right, our team is one message away. For tips on avoiding rental scams, see our blog.

Our values

  • Simplicity — searching, listing and paying should take minutes, not days.
  • Fairness — clear costs, no hidden fees, and commission only when a sale truly happens.
  • Local first — built for how Kenyans actually search, pay and move.

Built for Kenya

From Nairobi's estates to the coast and beyond, NestKey is designed around how Kenyans actually search for homes; by neighbourhood, with real photos, honest locations and M‑Pesa at the centre. As an installable web app, NestKey works on any smartphone — add it to your home screen and search on the go, wherever you are in the country.
