Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information NestKey collects, why, who sees it, how long we keep it and what you can do about it. We wrote it in plain language. It follows Kenya’s Data Protection Act, 2019 and its regulations.

The short version

  • Listings are public. When you post a property, its details, photos, location and your name and phone number are shown to everyone so house hunters can reach you.
  • Title deed numbers stay private. They are only used to answer “does this number match a listing?” on our Verify a deed page.
  • When you book or enquire, your name, phone number and message go to the person who listed the property.
  • Our servers are outside Kenya. We ask for your consent before storing property details there.
  • No ads, no tracking, no selling your data.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time, and you can complain to the Data Commissioner.

NestKey (“we”, “us”) runs the property website nestkey.co.ke and its web app. We decide how and why your personal data is used, which makes us the data controller under the Data Protection Act.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only as follows:

  • The public: everything on a published listing, including the lister’s name and phone number.
  • Listers: receive the bookings and enquiries you send them. After that, the lister handles your details. NestKey does not control what they do with them.
  • Buyers: see a seller’s payout details only when they choose to pay for a property that is for sale.
  • Service providers who run parts of NestKey for us:
    • Render, which hosts our website, app and database.
    • Our cloud storage provider, which stores photos and videos.
    • Pesapal, which processes payments by M-Pesa, Airtel Money, card and bank.
    • Google, for “Continue with Google” sign-in and for the fonts the site uses.
    • OpenStreetMap, which provides map images.
    • unpkg, which serves the map software.
    These providers receive what they need to do their job, which for fonts and maps is usually only your IP address.
  • Social media: when a listing goes live we may promote it on NestKey’s own social media pages. This uses the listing’s title, price, area, description, a photo and a link. Your phone number and title deed number are never included.
  • WhatsApp: if you tap a WhatsApp button, WhatsApp (Meta) handles that conversation under its own privacy policy.
  • Authorities: when the law requires it, or to protect people from fraud or harm.
  • A buyer of our business: if NestKey is sold or merged, on the same terms as this policy.

5. Property details and title deeds

The Data Protection Act treats property details as sensitive personal data, which means they need extra care. NestKey’s purpose is to publish property details, so here is exactly how we handle them:

  • You decide what to publish on a listing, including how precise the map pin is. You can edit or delete a listing at any time.
  • We ask for your express consent before storing a listing (section 7).
  • Title deed numbers are never shown publicly. On the Verify a deed page, someone who already has a number can check whether it matches a live listing. If it does, they see only that listing’s public details: its title, area, price and the lister’s name. The check is rate-limited to stop people guessing numbers.
  • Your national ID number and KRA PIN are visible only to you and NestKey staff.

6. Storage outside Kenya

Our hosting, database and media storage providers run their servers outside Kenya. So when you use NestKey, your personal data, including the property details on your listings, is transferred to and stored in other countries.

We protect it by:

  • using established providers with security commitments;
  • encrypting all traffic between your device and NestKey (HTTPS);
  • limiting who can access the data;
  • keeping title deed and ID numbers off public pages.

Because property details are sensitive personal data, we ask for your express consent to this transfer when you post a listing (section 7). We keep a record of when you gave it.

7. Your consent, and withdrawing it

When you post a property you tick a box agreeing that we may store your listing, including the property’s details and location, on servers outside Kenya. You can withdraw that consent at any time by deleting the listing from your dashboard, or by asking us to delete it. Withdrawing doesn’t affect anything we did before. It does mean the listing comes down, and we don’t refund fees for the time already used.

9. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your data is used (this policy);
  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct data that is wrong or misleading;
  • delete data that is no longer needed, or that we have no right to keep;
  • object to processing, including processing based on our legitimate interests;
  • restrict processing while a concern is looked into;
  • data portability: receive your data in a common electronic format;
  • withdraw consent at any time (section 7).

You can see and edit most of your data yourself in your dashboard. For anything else, email support@nestkey.co.ke with “Privacy request” in the subject. We may need to confirm your identity first. We aim to reply to access requests within 7 days, to correction, deletion, restriction and objection requests within 14 days, and to portability requests within 30 days. If we can’t do what you ask, for example because the law requires us to keep a payment record, we’ll explain why.

10. Complaints

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your data, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC), Kenya’s data protection regulator, at www.odpc.go.ke.

11. Security

Our safeguards include:

  • encrypted connections (HTTPS);
  • hashed passwords;
  • sign-in tokens that expire;
  • access controls, so people can only change their own listings and bookings;
  • limits on repeated requests;
  • keeping private details, such as deed and ID numbers, out of public pages.

No system is perfectly secure. If a data breach is likely to put you at risk, we will tell the Data Commissioner within 72 hours of finding out, tell you as soon as we reasonably can, and explain what you can do to protect yourself.

12. Children

NestKey is for adults. You must be 18 or older to create an account, post a listing or make a booking. We don’t knowingly collect data from children. If you think a child has given us their details, contact us and we’ll delete them.

13. Changes to this policy

When we change how we use personal data, we update this page and the date at the top. If a change is significant, for example a new kind of data or a new reason for using it, we’ll also tell signed-in users on the site before it takes effect.
